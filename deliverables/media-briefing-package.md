## 6. Legal Framework Summary

*Only **VERIFIED** or **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** items from `legal-research-memo.md` are summarized. **COULD NOT VERIFY** authorities are omitted; **Beshears v. USD 305** is **not** included. **Additional legal authorities are under review by counsel.***

### Kansas: discipline and school property (K.S.A. 72-6114) — **VERIFIED**

Grounds for suspension/expulsion include conduct that **endangers safety** or **invades rights** **“at school, on school property, or at a school supervised activity”** and similar **location-based** phrasing for other subdivisions—**no time-of-day limit appears in the quoted subsections** [SOURCE: legal-research-memo | K.S.A. 72-6114 | VERIFIED | https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch72/072_061_0014.html].

### Kansas: bullying policies/plans (K.S.A. 72-6147) — **VERIFIED**

Districts must adopt a **policy** and **plan** addressing bullying **“on or while utilizing school property, in a school vehicle or at a school-sponsored activity or event”** [SOURCE: legal-research-memo | K.S.A. 72-6147 | VERIFIED | https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch72/072_061_0047.html]. After-school use of **school property** implicates the **“on… school property”** language.

### Kansas Tort Claims Act / recreational use (K.S.A. 75-6104(a)(15)) — **PARTIALLY VERIFIED**

The **immunity text** for injuries from use of property intended/permitted as **park, playground, or open recreational** area is **VERIFIED** as quoted in the memo. **Caveat:** The memo **did not verify** a statutory **“fee-based childcare exclusion”** inside (a)(15); that framing is an **argument**, not confirmed statutory language [SOURCE: legal-research-memo | PARTIALLY VERIFIED].

### KORA (K.S.A. 45-215, 45-217, 45-218(d)) — **VERIFIED**

Short title, **public agency/public record** definitions, and **third business day** response standard are **VERIFIED** [SOURCE: legal-research-memo]. **Caveat:** Whether **JCPRD** is a **public agency** for every record type may need **entity-specific** confirmation with counsel [memo note].

### KDHE childcare licensing — **VERIFIED** (selected regulations)

- **K.A.R. 28-4-420**: **child care center** definition includes care **≥ three hours/day**, various hours [SOURCE: legal-research-memo | VERIFIED].  
- **K.A.R. 28-4-115a**: supervision; providers must know **location** and **activities**; **outdoor** rule for children **under five**—**within sight and proximity** [SOURCE: legal-research-memo | VERIFIED].  
- **K.A.R. 28-4-114**: staffing/group size tables **located**; memo did not reproduce numbers [SOURCE: legal-research-memo | VERIFIED — consult source for figures].
