# HeyGen Video Script — Working Draft

---

Let me tell you how the system actually works.

Public institutions have the rhetoric down. Partnership. Safety. Accountability. They put it on their websites, in their marketing, in the handbooks they hand you at registration. And for most parents, that's enough. You drop your kid off and trust that what they told you is true.

It's not.

My son was assaulted at an after-school program run inside his school. He's six. Five staff were outside. None of them saw it. He'd been telling us about bullying for months — we reported it seven months earlier — but we dismissed it because we kept getting assurances it couldn't be happening. I failed my son by buying into the marketing hype.

His pediatrician filed a mandatory report with Kansas DCF. The program rushed an incident report to cover themselves, lawyered up, and went silent.

We went to the school district. They told us it wasn't their problem — "separate entity." But their own lease requires that program to abide by all rules, regulations, and policies adopted by the Board of Education. They market togetherness and disclaim responsibility. And they count on you not having the resources to call bullshit.

My son probably won't see justice from this. I've made peace with that. What I can't make peace with is the dishonesty — a system where the first instinct isn't "how do we fix this" but "how do we make this go away." That's an integrity failure.

So my son asked me to do something about it. He's six, and his instinct was to help other kids. That's more moral clarity than every administrator we've dealt with, combined.

This site exists because of him. Every statute, every email, every policy contradiction — documented, sourced, and public. Not because we want sympathy. Because you deserve to have what we didn't. Honesty.

If you're going through this — reach out to me. We're building a free tool to do for every parent what I had to do from scratch. It's not ready yet, but it's coming. And I'll help you in the meantime.

Show up at school board meetings. File complaints — because complaints create records, records create patterns, and patterns create accountability. Demand transparency. Don't accept non-answers.

The system won't fix itself. It's not designed to. It's designed to exhaust you into silence.

But silence is a choice. And we're choosing to be loud.

Thank you.
