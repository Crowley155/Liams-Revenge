## Design Context

### Users
USDWatch serves parents and families navigating institutional failure in public school systems. They are often stressed, time-constrained, and trying to turn messy facts, emails, policies, and records into a coherent case file.

### Brand Personality
Evidence-backed, steady, and parent-first. The tone should feel serious without being cold, urgent without being alarmist, and practical rather than performative.

### Aesthetic Direction
The interface should feel like a careful case desk: structured, readable, and grounded in source material. Use a restrained product UI for private workflows and a clear editorial voice for public pages. Avoid glossy SaaS hype, legal-tech intimidation, and dark-mode spectacle.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the action a parent needs to take next.
- Make evidence strength, gaps, and uncertainty visible instead of burying them.
- Keep the public promise clear: free case evaluation, with organization plans funding infrastructure.
- Preserve the existing advocacy/demo case while keeping private workspaces clearly scoped.
- Favor dense, calm, scannable product surfaces over decorative marketing layouts.
